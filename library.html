Every dimension is a native Meta-accepted size. No upscaling on ingest.

Read this before uploading — the two rules that cause cropping

  • Never put a 9:16 file into a Feed placement. Feed accepts 1.91:1 down to 4:5 only. 4:5 is the tallest ratio Feed will show — a 1080 × 1920 file dropped into Feed gets centre-cropped to 4:5 and loses the top and bottom of the card. Use the 4:5 or 1:1 files for Feed, the 9:16 files for Stories and Reels.
  • Stories and Reels do not share the same safe zone. Reels overlays far more chrome at the bottom — the caption, engagement rail and CTA eat roughly the bottom 672 px versus about 340 px on Stories. A file that is safe on Stories can still be covered on Reels. That is why this pack ships them as two separate files, each built to its own reserve, instead of one 9:16 for both.

Verification method. Every one of the 27 files was downloaded back from this live site and re-measured by reading the raw PNG IHDR header bytes — not by trusting the renderer that produced it, and not by trusting the filename. Dimensions, colour type and bit depth were confirmed independently, and each file's safe-zone pass is recorded per-file in render_report.json.

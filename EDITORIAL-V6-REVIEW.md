# Julie masterclass: new editorial direction

Review only. No Meta upload or production changes.

## Why the earlier direction failed

The previous revisions adjusted font sizes without fixing the visual structure. Repeated serif blocks, two competing claims, small utility text and a decorative price created too many focal points. Inherited CSS overrides also caused inconsistent layout behavior. Automated overlap checks were necessary but did not establish good art direction.

## New direction

Concept: presenter-led editorial advertising with one dominant promise and one consistent enrollment strip.

References: Apple campaign hierarchy, MasterClass presenter-led photography and Vogue editorial type scale. These are composition references, not claims of equivalent quality.

- Blank stylesheet, no inherited earlier banner CSS.
- Original Playfair Display, original Playfair Display Italic and original Inter files only.
- Standing portrait: monumental “Age slower” promise, separated from the supporting authority claim.
- Zoom: large event title and full, uncropped original Zoom illustration.
- Blue portrait: edge-to-edge editorial blue text plane instead of a rounded floating flyer card.
- Compact $49, unified dates and enrollment information.
- Removed exclusivity line. No new decorative pills.
- Three concepts in blue, gold and white, 1080 × 1350 only. Other placement adaptation awaits approval.

The world-ranking language is user-requested campaign wording also present on the [masterclass page](https://www.longevitylifeacademy.com/julie-masterclass/), not an independently verified current worldwide ranking. The original Zoom artwork remains illustrative.
